How it works

  1. Data — scikit-learn's 1,797 handwritten digits, each 8×8 pixels.
  2. Train two models — an SVM and a 200-tree Random Forest on the same split.
  3. Export — export_web_model.py saves the SVM's 705 support vectors.
  4. Predict in the browser — your drawing is scaled to 8×8 and 45 pairwise classifiers vote.

Hand-drawn digits look different from the scanned training set, so drawings are harder than the test images. That gap is real, and it's part of what this demo shows.
