HOW TO PLAY

  1. LEVEL 1 · DATAscikit-learn's 1,797 handwritten digits, each 8×8 pixels.
  2. LEVEL 2 · TRAINAn SVM and a 200-tree Random Forest on the same split.
  3. LEVEL 3 · EXPORTexport_web_model.py saves the SVM's 705 support vectors.
  4. BOSS · PREDICTYour drawing is scaled to 8×8 and 45 pairwise classifiers vote.

Hand-drawn digits look different from the scanned training set, so drawings are harder than test images. That gap is real, and it's part of what this demo shows.
